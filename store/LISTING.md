## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
One phone. One secret word. One impostor sitting right next to you.

Impostor is a social deduction party game for 3 to 20 players sharing a single
phone. Everyone gets the same secret word — everyone except the impostors, who
see nothing and have to blend in.

HOW IT WORKS

Pass the phone around the circle. Each player looks at the screen alone and taps
to hide it before handing it on. Then you go around giving one clue each about
the word. Say too little and you look suspicious. Say too much and you hand the
word to the impostor. Finally everyone votes in secret, and the app reveals the
word, the impostors, and who fooled who.

A round takes about five minutes. Explaining the rules takes under one.

WHAT YOU GET

• 760 family-friendly words across 25 categories — food, sports, movies, animals,
  countries, cities, superheroes, video games, drinks, jobs, nature and more
• Choose 1 to several impostors — the game never lets them outnumber the crew
• Optional impostor hints, in two strengths: a clear nudge like "Italian food",
  or something cryptic like "Round and shared"
• Three difficulty levels
• Optional discussion and voting timers
• Custom player names
• Secret pass-the-phone voting, or skip it and decide out loud
• Dark and light themes
• Sound, music and haptics, all optional

COMPLETELY OFFLINE

Impostor works perfectly in Airplane Mode. There is no account, no sign-in, no
cloud, no server. The entire word list ships inside the app.

BUILT FOR PRIVACY

The app requests no permissions at all — not even internet access. It collects no
data, contains no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Your settings and a short
list of recently used words stay on your device and are deleted when you
uninstall.

PERFECT FOR

Parties, road trips, pubs, classrooms, family dinners, waiting rooms, camping
trips and anywhere else a group has five minutes and one phone between them.
```
