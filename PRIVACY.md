# Privacy Policy — Impostor

**Last updated: 25 August 2026**

## The short version

Impostor collects nothing, sends nothing, and cannot. The app does not request the
`INTERNET` permission, so it has no way to transmit anything anywhere, even by
accident.

## What data the app collects

None.

Impostor does not collect, store or transmit personal data. There is no account, no
sign-in, no profile, no email address, no advertising identifier, no analytics SDK and
no crash-reporting SDK.

## What is stored on your device

Two things, both in the app's own private storage, and both removed completely when
you uninstall the app:

1. **Your settings** — sound, music, vibration, theme, and your preferred defaults for
   players, impostors, category, difficulty, hints and timers.
2. **A short list of recently used words** — up to 80 entries, kept only so the game
   does not hand you the same word twice in a row. You can clear it at any time from
   Settings → *Clear used-word history*.

Nothing about a round — the secret word, who was the impostor, who voted for whom — is
written to disk. It exists only in memory while you are playing.

If you have Android's backup enabled, these preferences may be included in your own
Google account backup, which is controlled by you and by Google, not by this app.

## Permissions

Impostor requests **no permissions at all**. In particular it does not request
internet access, storage, location, contacts, camera, microphone or vibration.
Haptic feedback uses the standard system view-feedback API, which needs no permission.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it collects
no data from children either.

## Third parties

There are none. No advertising networks, no analytics providers, no cloud services, no
third-party SDKs beyond Google's own AndroidX and Jetpack Compose UI libraries, which
run entirely on your device.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes what it stores, this policy will be
updated before that version is published, and the change will be described here.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer address listed on the app's
Google Play store page.
